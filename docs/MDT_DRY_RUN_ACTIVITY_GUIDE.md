# MDT Dry Run Activity Guide

Purpose: help UNICEF, LGU, DSWD, and field case workers experience the Kalinga CEFMU system as a shared case management tool.

Recommended time: 60 to 75 minutes  
Group size: 5 to 7 participants per group  
Device setup: one laptop per group is enough

## Facilitation Design

This should feel like a practical MDT huddle, not a computer exam.

Use three simple rules:

1. One person drives the computer.
2. Everyone else acts from a role card.
3. The group must agree before anything is recorded in the system.

## Roles Per Group

| Role | Main question |
|---|---|
| Case Worker / Social Worker | What must be recorded today, and what follow-up is needed? |
| LGU SWDO / Implementer | What local support and monitoring can the LGU provide? |
| Health / Medical | Is there a medical or psychosocial concern needing referral? |
| Education / DepEd | How can schooling or ALS continuity be protected? |
| Legal / PNP-WCPD / PAO | Is legal protection, documentation, or safety action needed? |
| Data Privacy Observer | Is the group exposing only the data needed for the task? |
| System Recorder | Enters the agreed note, referral, or service in the system. |

## Fictional Case Packet

Case ID: `TRAINING-CEFMU-001`  
Client: 16-year-old girl, Grade 9 learner  
Area: Isabela, Region II - Cagayan Valley  
Case type: Early union, possible forced marriage risk  
Source: School focal person and barangay referral  
Connectivity context: field area has unstable mobile data; case worker synced after returning to signal coverage

Case narrative:

The client has missed several weeks of classes. A school focal person reported that the client is living with an older partner after family pressure to enter a union. The client appears anxious and has avoided contact with some relatives. The barangay says the family wants the matter handled quietly. The case worker completed initial intake and needs the MDT to agree on immediate next steps.

Known information:

- Client is under 18.
- Client may be at risk of further pressure from family and partner.
- School attendance has stopped.
- No financial assistance has been provided yet.
- No medical examination has been recorded yet.
- No legal consultation has been documented yet.
- LGU can assist with local monitoring and family conference.

Unknowns to clarify:

- Whether the client is currently safe.
- Whether there is pregnancy, injury, or medical concern.
- Whether the client consents to specific support options.
- Whether legal protection or WCPD documentation is immediately needed.
- Whether school re-entry or ALS is the best education pathway.

## User Stories For The Dry Run

1. As a Case Worker, I need to open a CEFMU case and record an MDT progress note so that the next worker can understand what was agreed.
2. As an LGU implementer, I need to see cases assigned to my area so that I can monitor follow-up and service delivery.
3. As a DSWD Field Office user, I need to review regional case progress so that referrals do not get lost between agencies.
4. As a CPU monitor, I need a read-only monitoring view so that I can track assigned cases without editing restricted data.
5. As an MDT member, I need referral options that match my function so that the recorded purpose is accurate.
6. As a data privacy observer, I need names and sensitive details protected so that the system follows minimum necessary disclosure.

## Activity Steps

### Part 1: MDT Huddle

Time: 10 minutes

Each group answers:

- What is the immediate risk or concern?
- Which MDT member or agency receives the first referral?
- What service is actually provided today?
- What next step must be monitored by LGU or DSWD?

### Part 2: System Recording

Time: 15 to 20 minutes

Use the Case Worker account or a facilitator-provided demo case.

Tasks:

1. Open the assigned case.
2. Add an MDT referral or progress note.
3. Select the referred MDT member or agency.
4. Choose only the matching referral purpose.
5. Record action taken and next steps.
6. Add a service provided.
7. If the service is Financial, enter the amount. If not Financial, leave amount hidden.

### Part 3: Monitoring Check

Time: 10 minutes

Switch or observe another role view:

- LGU Supervisor: Can the assigned area case be found?
- Field Office: Can regional monitoring see the case?
- CPU Monitor: Is the view read-only?
- Admin: Is intake hidden from administrator modules?

### Part 4: Debrief

Time: 20 minutes

Ask each group to give one answer in each column:

| Keep | Improve | Add later for CPS expansion |
|---|---|---|
| What works for field use? | What confused users? | What is needed for non-CEFMU child protection cases? |

## Suggested Facilitator Prompts

- What did the system make easier compared with a spreadsheet?
- Where did the group hesitate?
- Was the referral purpose clear enough?
- Was any information unnecessary for the role viewing it?
- What would make this easier in a GIDA barangay?
- What should be required before closing a case?

## Non-Technical Participant Support

Before the activity:

- Tell participants they are not being tested on computer skills.
- Assign one system recorder per group.
- Give printed role cards or write roles on paper.
- Let people discuss first, then record the group decision.
- Use plain words: "case note", "referral", "service provided", "next step".

During the activity:

- Avoid explaining every menu.
- Ask: "What decision do you want the system to remember?"
- Let the facilitator or recorder handle navigation when needed.

## Success Criteria

The activity is successful if participants can:

- Explain the case flow from intake to closure.
- Identify which role should perform intake.
- Record one MDT referral or progress note.
- Record one actual service provided.
- Confirm LGU/DSWD monitoring value.
- Name at least three improvements before future CPS expansion.
